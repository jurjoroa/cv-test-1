Experience

Software Developer Associate — Stanford University School of Medicine

10/2024 – Present · Palo Alto, California, US

Software development of a colorectal cancer simulation model for the Cancer Intervention and Surveillance Modeling Network (CISNET) program, focusing on cost-effectiveness analysis and decision modeling. Collaboration with the National Cancer Institute (NIH) and a multidisciplinary team to develop colorectal cancer screening and treatment models. Adapted the SimCRC model for Chile to optimize FIT screening and developed a cost-effective screening program for high-risk individuals in Mexico. Built dynamic Shiny applications and Quarto reports for interactive data visualization. Reduced calibration execution time by 60% by replacing full 0–100 age loops with a difference-array/cumsum algorithm.

Research Assistant — Hertie School Data Science Lab

11/2023 – 07/2024 · Berlin, Germany

Built automated webscrapers and crawlers to collect real-time data from Twitter, enabling early detection of flu and allergy-related trends. Extracted and structured political speeches and online content for narrative-framing analysis. Constructed a comprehensive database of international organizations by scraping official websites. Tagged causal relationships in political and health-related texts to support training datasets for large language models.

Data Analyst — CISIDAT (Health Research Consortium)

09/2020 – 10/2024 · Remote (Mexico)

Worked on optimization of cost-effectiveness algorithms for colorectal cancer and migrated natural-history, screening, and surveillance models from C++ to R. Estimated the infection force of Helicobacter pylori in the U.S. population using catalytic models on NHANES data. Built a Shiny app and website to visualize H. pylori force of infection. Determined cumulative COVID-19 mortality risk across Mexico’s population using survival models. Conducted twenty-year homicide trend analyses for Mexico using Bayesian methods.

Research Assistant, Drug Policy Program — Center for Research and Teaching in Economics

01/2017 – 06/2021 · Aguascalientes, Mexico

Created a dataset locating and characterizing 148 criminal groups in Mexico using web scraping (Python) and text analysis (R) on press and social-media reporting. Designed and programmed an electronic survey to study harm reductions associated with psychoactive substances. Coordinated a team that geocoded over 10,000 addresses related to executions, confrontations, attacks, and arrests using Python.
